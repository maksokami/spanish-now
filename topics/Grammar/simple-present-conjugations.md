# Present Tense Conjugations

In Spanish, verbs are categorized by their endings in the infinitive form: **-ar**, **-er**, and **-ir**. To conjugate a regular verb in the present tense, you drop the infinitive ending and add the appropriate ending based on the subject pronoun.

## Regular -AR Verbs (e.g., *Hablar* - to speak)

| Pronoun | Ending | Example (*Hablar*) | Translation |
|---------|--------|-------------------|-------------|
| Yo | -o | habl**o** | I speak |
| Tú | -as | habl**as** | You speak |
| Él, Ella, Usted | -a | habl**a** | He/She/You speak |
| Nosotros/as | -amos | habl**amos** | We speak |
| Vosotros/as | -áis | habl**áis** | You all speak |
| Ellos, Ellas, Ustedes | -an | habl**an** | They/You all speak |

## Regular -ER Verbs (e.g., *Comer* - to eat)

| Pronoun | Ending | Example (*Comer*) | Translation |
|---------|--------|-------------------|-------------|
| Yo | -o | com**o** | I eat |
| Tú | -es | com**es** | You eat |
| Él, Ella, Usted | -e | com**e** | He/She/You eat |
| Nosotros/as | -emos | com**emos** | We eat |
| Vosotros/as | -éis | com**éis** | You all eat |
| Ellos, Ellas, Ustedes | -en | com**en** | They/You all eat |

## Regular -IR Verbs (e.g., *Vivir* - to live)

| Pronoun | Ending | Example (*Vivir*) | Translation |
|---------|--------|-------------------|-------------|
| Yo | -o | viv**o** | I live |
| Tú | -es | viv**es** | You live |
| Él, Ella, Usted | -e | viv**e** | He/She/You live |
| Nosotros/as | -imos | viv**imos** | We live |
| Vosotros/as | -ís | viv**ís** | You all live |
| Ellos, Ellas, Ustedes | -en | viv**en** | They/You all live |
